# Shafranov

A real-time, free-boundary tokamak plasma simulator for [TCV](https://www.epfl.ch/research/domains/swiss-plasma-center/research/tcv/),
the Tokamak à Configuration Variable at EPFL, written in C++20.

It solves the Grad–Shafranov equation on TCV's coil and vessel geometry and evolves the coil, vessel and plasma
currents in time, so the vertical instability comes out of the equations rather than being scripted.

**Status:** free-boundary equilibria and the inverse shape solve agree with FreeGS; coil, vessel and plasma
currents evolve in time and the vertical instability emerges from the circuit equations. Feedback control is next.

![The live window: a lower single-null TCV plasma, its separatrix and X-point, and the E and F coil currents the
inverse solve chose](docs/window.png)

## Build

Needs CMake 3.24+, Ninja and a C++20 compiler (gcc 13+ tested).

```sh
cmake -S . -B build -G Ninja
cmake --build build
ctest --test-dir build --output-on-failure
```

The live window (`build/src/app/shafranov_app`) needs OpenGL 3.3; pass `-DSHAFRANOV_APP=OFF` to build without it.
Drag the plasma shape and the inverse solve picks the coil currents, or switch to fixed currents and watch an
elongated plasma lose its vertical position.

## Validation

Every claim the simulator makes is backed by a test:

| Check | Status |
|---|---|
| Solov'ev analytic equilibrium, second-order convergence | passing |
| Green's functions against analytic limits | passing |
| Code-to-code against FreeGS on TCV geometry | passing: axis 0.01 mm, X-point 0.07 mm, coil currents 0.02% |
| Coil self-inductances against Marchioni (EPFL thesis, 2024) | passing: OH1 6.92 / 6.9 mH, F 7.44 / 7.4 mH, E 1.74 / 1.7 mH |
| Vessel current decay times against Hofmann et al. (1996) | passing: 13.4 / 14.0 ms (even), 8.1 / 8.6 ms (odd) |
| Vertical instability: grows, faster with elongation | passing: 445, 843, 1857 1/s at kappa 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 |
| Vertical growth rate of a specific TCV shot | not yet: needs that shot's reconstructed shape; TCV's measured rates are a few hundred to ~1500 1/s, the same range |

## Machine data

`data/tcv/` holds TCV's real geometry, regenerated by `tools/make_tcv_data.py` from pinned sources:

- **Coils** (32, in 20 circuits): positions, sizes and turns from the RZIP TCV structure model, identical to
  appendix tables 2 and 3 of [arXiv:1301.7617](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.7617). Resistances follow from the
  conductor cross-sections and match the values in S. Marchioni's EPFL thesis (2024).
- **Vacuum vessel**: the 256-filament stainless steel model from
  [RZIP](https://github.com/atisharma/rzip) (J. Wainwright, A. Sharma), the same model TCV's own codes use.
- **First wall**: the LIUQE limiter contour of shot 65402 from the
  [TCV-X21 dataset](https://github.com/SPCData/TCV-X21) (Oliveira, Body et al., Nucl. Fusion 62 (2022) 096001,
  CC-BY-4.0).
